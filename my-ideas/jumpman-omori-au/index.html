Ideas for Jumpman - a silly Omori AU

NOTE: spoilers for Omori, which are rather big considering how impactful stuff is in there

This text is just copied from the Reddit post

I'm not even going to try to structure this this time, I'm just gonna lay out the ideas as I go because I was thinking about this for days

You start as Jumpman...okay this is going worse than I thought

I don't really know what to imagine but I guess in Headspace Jumpman will go on various adventures without much of a care in the world. At the start, Bowser comes in and kidnaps Peach, then something happens. They don't know yet what Bowser is going to do with her, but I guess Green Jumpman will look at something, become horrified and barely before you know what's going on Jumpman will go back to the hub and be forced to eat a poison mushroom.

In the real world, Mario wakes up in the middle of the night and discovers that a pipe came loose I guess. I dunno what goes in this sequence but yeah, something's gotta happen.

In Headspace things go the same way as usual. They go on adventures checking every castle they can see only for a few Toads to say Peach must be in another castle. There's gonna be a lot of side characters and whatever, so basically all the Mario characters in history will probably show up somewhere or other. The only person I can think to substitute Mari is Rosalina because Peach will be gone and I dunno who else owns a hospice. Obviously Mikal and Neb will be Wario and Waluigi. I guess Toad and Toadette will also be in your party. Some activities you can do with your friends include go-karting, tennis, golf, and a bunch of other minigames. Do the rest yourself

In the real world, Mario wakes up with Luigi and I guess Toadsworth announces that Bowser kidnapped Peach, but this time he made/went to another hotel. Luigi's even more timid than before and basically makes excuses to "get prepared" and procrastinate, and Mario's just tagging along, barely cognisant of what's happening. Toad and Toadette probably don't understand what's happening but I guess they try to tag them along on a quest to save Peach. Of course, Mario can choose to not do anything or...book in at a couple of hotels, I guess. It's a long journey and the conditions won't be very good for staying anywhere other than there. Some fears that will show up include smoke, pennies, a hazardous malfunctioning toaster and I don't really know. Mario can do all sorts of jobs for a lot of quick coins rather than get some by himself I guess. Maybe he'd be paid in tokens instead.

I saw this from here and it fits way too much so this might even go in

Instead of Something, sometimes burnt pieces of toast will appear. The nearest person will quickly eat it, and then say they're going to be sick and run off somewhere. Their mangled-looking corpses can probably be found in the Underwhere or something.

I don't know what happens there but either Bowser can take over the world with Peach while Mario either eats a poison mushroom in real life, or does who-knows-what, or Mario can overcome his struggles and infiltrate the hotel. I guess Bowser would beat Mario by surprise after a climactic fight, and then he'd just lay there while Luigi chases Bowser somewhere or whatever. Still a lot of conceptual gaps to fill but he can either succumb to his demise or get some resolve.

Hmm I think maybe Peach will at some point become desperate to try to escape, but if she jumps out in the harsh weather at Bowser's last lair her umbrella will break and she'll fall to her death so you might want to stop that from happening. But Peach will also generally appear like Spirit Mari did.

Alright, another much more interesting thought I had today. I just realised a lot of the fear Mario could have could come from realising he's being toyed with, since Hotel Mario was very interactive with the player, which was very important and should've thought of that earlier. So I think Mario is going to be helplessly trying to fight a lot of impulses, maybe trick the player so they don't end up having an opportunity/thought to pull off something nasty, and have the game in general incite a lot of emotional attachment from the player. So that's another thing he'll be trying to repress in the face of all the "Thank you for playing" messages from Nintendo, which would have a lot of interesting dynamics.

I think in the back of his mind Mario would be trying to appease you, thinking if he just follows along (which he's usually okay with) you won't do anything crazy. However now he's going to have a lot of conflicting feelings, really not wanting to go back to any hotel while you try to progress the game. This Mario game in particular would have consequential choices following from there.

I think the actual game would have a combination of platforming, exploration and RPG elements

Instead of Omoli we have the Mario bootleg from The Simpsons Game
